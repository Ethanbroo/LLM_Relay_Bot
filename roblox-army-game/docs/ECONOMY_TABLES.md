### 6.1 Time-skip cash price (what your shops would pay right now)

sales = floor(seconds / sale interval); with an employee every sale is paid, without one

Random offers only show a time skip when the preview is >= 25% of its reference;

## 11. Pacing estimate

Model: the player enters area a with the reference upgrades of area a. To finish the
area they buy its 4 slots, raise all 3 runner upgrades to the next reference level, and
pay the gate. Income per run = efficiency x R(area) + passive income of every owned
level-1 section for one run (sections assumed kept stocked at the same efficiency).
The cheapest remaining purchase is bought as soon as it is affordable. Combat income
and section upgrades are ignored (so real play is a bit faster). Note: one wrong troop /
weapon lane loses the boss (see 2.2), so 75% is a good but not perfect player.
