# Block Santos

A free browser game: Minecraft-style creative building in a GTA-style voxel city.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No install, no account, no cost.
It loads the free Three.js library from cdnjs, so it needs an internet connection.

- Fly or walk, place and break blocks, copy blocks with middle click
- Traffic that stops at your walls, cars you can get into and drive (E)
- TNT (slot 9) that blows up blocks and launches cars
- Day / sunset / night (T), round radar map, beach and pier
- Works on phones with on-screen controls
- Your builds are saved in your browser

`game.html` is the same game without the outer `<html>`/`<head>` wrapper (used for the hosted version).
